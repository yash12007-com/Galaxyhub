Announcements

  • Android app distribution program will be discontinued from Oct 1, 2026.

    • As part of our evolving platform strategy, the Android app distribution channel on Paradise SDP will be retired on October 1, 2026. Developers currently publishing Android apps should migrate to Windows software distribution before the deadline. Existing Android submissions will remain listed with reduced visibility as new policy restrictions take effect. No action is needed for your Windows-based applications.

  • New: Windows desktop publishing is now open for all developers.

    • Developers can now publish Windows applications directly on the Galaxyhub marketplace through Paradise SDP. Submit your software with full pricing control, regional availability, and automatic license delivery to buyers worldwide.

What Galaxyhub offers

  • Global software distribution

    • Publish once and reach users across every region. Galaxyhub handles licensing, delivery, and visibility so you can focus on building great software.

  • Developer-first publishing tools

    • A streamlined publishing pipeline lets you upload, price, and manage your applications from a single dashboard. Set regional pricing, promotional discounts, and download limits with no technical overhead.

  • Instant license delivery

    • When a user purchases your software, their download license is issued instantly. No delays, no manual fulfillment — the platform takes care of the entire transaction lifecycle.

  • Built-in marketplace visibility

    • Every published app is indexed and searchable across Galaxyhub. Categories, tags, and search ensure your software is discoverable by the right audience without additional marketing spend.

Why Galaxyhub?

  • Part of the Paradise SDP ecosystem

    • Galaxyhub is the public marketplace layer of Paradise SDP (Software Distribution Platform), built to help individual developers, organisations, and businesses expand their software reach. Your applications gain visibility in every online region, and users can purchase download licenses directly through Galaxyhub — backed by Paradise SDP's distribution infrastructure.

  • No payment restrictions

    • We support multiple payment methods so developers can publish on the Yash12007 Store, Galaxyhub marketplace, and other Paradise SDP endpoints without geographic or payment-method limitations holding back their distribution.

  • Transparent licensing

    • Every transaction on Galaxyhub includes clear license terms visible to buyers at the point of purchase. Developers define their own licensing models — perpetual, subscription, or trial — and the platform enforces them automatically.

  • Zero upfront cost to publish

    • Listing your software on Galaxyhub is free. You only pay a small platform fee on successful transactions, so there's no financial barrier to getting your application in front of a global audience.
